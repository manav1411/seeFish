# Activity sources

Activity suggestions were checked on 20 September 2026. Links point to organisers or official directories and are used as discovery links; the model does not claim that an event is running on a particular future date.

| Activity or city | Verified source | What it supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| parkrun, all eight cities | [parkrun Australia](https://resources.parkrun.com/australia) | Free Saturday 5 km events, local event search, and volunteering. City labels in the model are search prompts; check the directory for the current course and start time. |
| Darwin | [Darwin parkrun](https://www.parkrun.com.au/darwin/) | Bicentennial Park course, Saturday timing, and volunteer information. |
| Melbourne parkrun | [Albert Melbourne parkrun](https://www.parkrun.com.au/albertmelbourne/) | Specific local event and volunteering page. |
| Sydney parkrun | [NSW Health: Centennial parkrun](https://www.activeandhealthy.nsw.gov.au/program/view/3489) | Public programme listing with registration and first-timer information. |
| Melbourne | [Gujarati Association of Victoria](https://gav.org.au/) | Gujarati cultural programming including Navratri and garba. |
| Sydney | [Gujarati Samaj](https://gujaratisamaj.org.au/) | Sydney Gujarati community and garba discovery link. |
| Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Canberra | [Holi Festival Australia](https://holifestival.au/) | Current organiser page describing Holi cultural events across these Australian cities and Melbourne/Sydney. |
| Hobart | [Indian Cultural Society of Tasmania](https://www.icstas.org.au/) | Indian cultural community hub, events, education, volunteering, and cultural programmes. |
| Darwin | [Northern Territory Government Indian community profile](https://nt.gov.au/community/multicultural-communities/multicultural-communities-and-religions/indian) | Indian community activities including India@Mindil, language classes, dance, music, and cultural learning. |
| Australia | [India.com.au](https://india.com.au/) | National Indian community events and city directory for broader discovery. |
| All cities | [Australian Government volunteering directory](https://www.dss.gov.au/volunteering/site/index.html) | Search volunteer opportunities by city and interest. |

The cultural suggestions intentionally avoid claims about attendee age, gender, relationship status, or likely romantic outcomes. “Attend repeatedly” is practical participation advice, not a prediction of matching probability. Garba is Gujarati and should be understood as one specific tradition within the wider range of Indian cultures.

Limitations: organiser calendars can change, and the source pages do not establish that an event is suitable for every newcomer or that a beginner session will be available. The interface therefore tells people to check the organiser and ask about introductory options. The parkrun city labels are prompts for the official directory, rather than claims about a particular course.
